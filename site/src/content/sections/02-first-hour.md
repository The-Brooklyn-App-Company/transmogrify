1. **Paste the start prompt.** It installs the pinned release and checks your
   machine without changing anything.
2. **Run the first run.** It asks for your choices (the app for your Oracle,
   its home, your timezone, models) and sets up the rest on your yes, then
   lists the few clicks only you can do.
3. **Start your Oracle.** One session, titled "Oracle", pinned. A test note
   proves mail reaches it.
4. **Add an operator.** Name it, pick its app, choose its models once.
5. **Send it a note from the other app.** Watch which delivery path it took.
6. **Hand a worker a task.** It runs in its own clone and hands back a commit
   you can fetch, review and merge.

Prefer a terminal? Clone the repository, check out the release tag
`v2.0.0`, then run `npm ci --ignore-scripts` and `./install.sh`.
